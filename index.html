Practical Knowledge

Electrical Safety & Buyer Guidance

Simple, essential guidelines to help you make safe and informed electrical purchases.

LED Brightness & Color

For bedrooms and living rooms, warm white (2700K–3000K) creates a cozy ambiance. For kitchens, studies, and shops, cool daylight (6500K) provides superior visual clarity.

Ceiling Fan Sizing

Rooms up to 100 sq. ft. typically require 900mm–1050mm sweep fans; standard 100–150 sq. ft. bedrooms perform best with 1200mm sweep models.

Switch & Socket Load Ratings

Use standard 6A sockets for phone chargers, TVs, and lamps. Dedicated 16A or 20A heavy-duty sockets are mandatory for geysers, air conditioners, and microwaves.

ISI-Marked Copper Wires

Always choose 100% electrolytic copper wires with genuine ISI marking and Flame Retardant (FR) insulation to prevent overheating and fire risks.

MCB vs. RCCB Protection

An MCB protects equipment from overloads and short circuits. An RCCB protects human lives from dangerous electric shock and earth leakage faults.

Always Power Off Before Testing

Always isolate the main power breaker before inspecting, replacing, or wiring any switch, socket, or fitting. Never handle electrical circuits with wet hands.

Safety Advisory: This guidance is for general informational awareness. All electrical installations, wiring modifications, and distribution board work must be executed by a trained, competent electrician.
